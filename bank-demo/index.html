One purchase, every step’s origin

A customer submits a 100-yuan fund purchase. The money is debited — does that mean the investment succeeded? When the fund company rejects it, why does a separate refund exist? How does each installment of a savings plan reuse those exact rules? This example answers those questions from a truth tree, not from prose.

SEMANTIC INVARIANTS

The tree pins the sentences people get wrong: payment success ≠ investment success · TA acceptance ≠ share confirmation · independent refund ≠ compensating a committed payment · saga ≠ pipeline. Each has rules and tests to back it.

Design projections

The tree also renders seven human-facing design views (journeys, screen flow, context map, event flow, cash experience/lifecycle, story map). They are derived design proposals — generated from truth, clearly labeled as such, never hand-maintained. Rendered figures land here in the next content pass.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Most tools make you choose: either a design canvas that drifts from the code, or code with no design view. A projection from the truth tree is neither — it is regenerable and readable, and its drift is detectable by re-rendering.
