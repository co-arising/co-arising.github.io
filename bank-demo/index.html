Bank wealth · a declaration guide

The payment went through.
Did the investment succeed?

Not yet. Payment, fund-company confirmation and a possible refund are different business facts. Follow their declarations to see why they need different consistency boundaries—and how each installment reuses them.

This page is a static guide, not a live bank backend or browser simulation. The linked graph is the shipped declaration snapshot. The separately tested local demo uses a mock transfer agent (TA), not a real fund-company connection.

The arrows below are a reading order through declarations, not an execution trace or proof that one graph edge causes the next action. Links select actual nodes in the 2026-11-11 SQLite snapshot F-03.

Payment

What did the debit actually establish?

The cash entry invokes a payment saga coordinating investor funds, the collection account and the order. A successful payment is not TA confirmation and does not establish ownership of fund shares.

Read the cash entry → Inspect the payment saga

TA rejection + refund

Why is the refund a new transaction?

The refund request checks the selected successful payment and a rejected TA status. It starts an independent refund business flow; it is not compensation for the already committed payment. The refund saga has its own outcomes.

TA rejection → Refund request guards → Independent refund saga

Bounded installments · SIP

How does an installment reuse the payment rules?

A sealed finite schedule and a retained occurrence identify what may be dispatched into the cash flow. Dispatch success is not investment success; correlated business terminals update installment results.

Seal the schedule → Admit an installment → Dispatch to cash

What does pausing change?

Read the paused-slot skip guards. This is explicit slot classification, not a policy of automatically pausing after three failures.

Unknown outcomes

What if the result is not known?

“Unknown” must not be relabeled “failed” or “succeeded.” TA observations and installment progress have explicit unknown declarations. The cash and SIP plans require reconciliation before resuming an unknown effect; a missing answer is not permission to repeat a debit.

Inspect TA unknown observation · Inspect installment unknown progress

KEEP THESE DISTINCTIONS
