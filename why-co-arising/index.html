Philosophy

Why co-arising, not ontology

In dependent origination, nothing has independent existence: things arise together, conditioned by their relations. That is exactly how domain knowledge behaves — and exactly why a static classification of “object types” keeps failing to capture it.

01

The problem we kept hitting

Two decades of the same wall, from opposite directions:

1999–2004 · research

“Business generates code” produced toys

Model-to-code research kept generating things that ran but couldn’t survive contact with a real domain: no enforceable rules, no traceability, no evolution path. The models were descriptions without consequences.

2019–2023 · industry

Compliance had to reverse-engineer lineage

Enterprises with hundreds of microservices had no data lineage. When legal asked “where did this number come from”, the only method was to grep the estate backwards. Knowledge existed only inside code — unreachable, unprovable.

Both walls have the same root: domain knowledge was treated as a documentation artifact of the code, instead of the other way around. The fix isn’t a better diagram tool. It’s making the domain description the thing that is compiled, verified and traced — so that every line of code provably comes from a named piece of knowledge.

02

DDD already knew: concepts co-arise

The name is not decoration — it is a reading of what DDD has said all along.

Tactical: an aggregate is a standing wave, not a class

Evans defined an aggregate as a consistency boundary. In a truth tree that becomes literal: an aggregate is what arises when its slices hold — each slice is one command, its guards, and the events it produces; the guards enforce the aggregate’s invariants; the events fold back into state. Remove any leg and the aggregate stops being an aggregate:

  • Commands without guards — mutation without rules; the invariant has no enforcement point.
  • Events without fold assignments — history that never becomes state.
  • Invariants nothing references — dead law; consistency exists on paper only.

The bank tree makes this concrete: CreditBalance exists because 27 slices carry guards like @guard[invariantRef](reject ACCOUNT_SEED_NONNEGATIVE …), backed by named @rule invariants — 149 of them across three aggregates verified.

Strategic: structure follows the business terrain

The strategic patterns are equally relational. Core / supporting / generic subdomains are decided by where the business has advantage or distinctive knowledge — not by any property of the code. A bounded context and its context map (customer–supplier, anticorruption layer, open host service…) co-arise from the org chart and the commercial landscape of upstream and downstream parties. In the truth-tree language these are first-class notes — context, subdomain, relation — joined by the same relation registry as everything else (R21 contexts-depending-on, R22 aggregate-belongs-to-subdomain).

THESIS

DDD’s core insight is co-arising. Aggregates, contexts and subdomains are not categories you file things into — they are structures that arise from relations (commands→events, invariants→guards, business terrain→context maps), persist while the relations hold, and dissolve when they don’t. Our language just makes that machine-checkable.

04

Ontology next door

The closest thing to this approach in industry is Palantir’s Ontology — and the differences are instructive, not hostile.

Ontology (proprietary)

A classification with an execution layer

  • Object types, link types, action types — a taxonomy of the enterprise, defined top-down in an Ontology Manager.
  • “Represents decisions, not just data” — a genuinely good line: actions write back through the ontology into datasets.
  • Closed: the execution layer, the semantics and the format are the product. No open interchange, no exit.
  • Meaning is declared once in the type system — objects keep sitting in their taxon even when every relationship that mattered has rotted.
vs
Co-arising (this project)

A relation graph that compiles

  • 22 typed relations with explicit impact flags (propagates) — meaning lives in the edges, and the edges are executable inputs.
  • Decisions-and-data too — but the action layer is generated open code (CQRS/ES Scala you can read, compile and leave with).
  • Open: MIT/Apache-2.0, file-based trees, SQLite graph, MCP access for any agent.
  • Meaning is continuously re-derived: the compiler re-proves the relations on every build; orphaned knowledge is detected, not archived.
ONE SENTENCE

In an ontology, an object is defined by its place in a taxonomy. In co-arising, a concept is defined by its relations — remove them and it stops meaning anything, and the tooling tells you exactly when that happened.

05

Two more commitments

Stewardship

Agents draft, humans decide

AI agents are extremely good at proposing domain structure and extremely bad at being accountable for it. So the contract is: agents may query, draft and fill; the truth tree is the only editing surface; every batch of edits must end with the validation gates green; approval is a human act recorded in provenance (approvedBy on every atom).

Human-verifiable production

People can still produce and check

The tree is YAML a person can read and edit. Validation is deterministic. Roundtrip is byte-identical. Machines do the volume — counting, compiling, anchoring, querying — and people stay responsible for correctness. That division of labor is a feature, not nostalgia.

06

What it is not, and why the odd name

Not

A low-code platform

No canvas of drag-and-drop blocks. The output is real, compiling, testable source code with a handwritten zone — because the hard half of every real system is exactly the part low-code can’t hold.

Not

A model-to-code toy

The acceptance bar is not “a demo runs”. It is: diff-able tree, byte-identical roundtrip, compiling generated project, anchors proven both directions — verified with commands, not vibes. See the gates ↗

Not

Another standard

Gherkin, AsyncAPI, OpenAPI, C4 are fine standards — the tree can project onto them. The owned layer is deliberately small: the domain IR and its relation registry. Projection table ↗

On the name: Palantir coined “Forward Deployed Engineer” in 2011, was widely told the name was absurd, kept it — and today FDE is a job category hundreds of companies hire for. Coining a word and holding it patiently is how a word becomes a category. We are holding co-arising.
